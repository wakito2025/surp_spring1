Graduate Education

Pioneering the urban and regional planning education in the Philippines, UP SURP offers innovative degree programs that cater to the students' varying academic aspirations. What sets UP SURP apart from its contemporaries is the delivery of specialized courses, allowing aspiring planners to direct their passion and contribution to specific planning sectors.

Training and Extension Services

Fulfilling its training and extension mandates, the School maintains a working relationship with many government inter-agency committees. legislative bodies, and local governments, and assists them with their planning and capacity building needs.

Research and Publication

UP SURP remains responsive to its research mandate through independent and collaborative studies that are not only geared towards knowledge-generation, but also translate into practice and policy-making.

SURP Library

The library provides a wide array of learning resources to support the School’s functions of conducting academic programs in planning; providing training courses, seminars, workshops in cooperation with other government agencies in carrying out research activities designed to solve problems of development particularly on settlement patterns and environmental studies.

The UP School of Urban and Regional Planning (UP SURP) through its Office of Training and Extension Services (OTES) congratulates the participants of the Special Course on Urban and Regional Planning (SCURP) 2026 for successfully completing the five-day online training program held on 11–15 May 2026.

Dean Dina C. Magnaye and Director Reynold Ferdinand G. Manegdeg represented UP SURP in a Congress hearing of the Committee on Government Enterprises and Privatization on the proposed bill establishing the Sierra Madre Conservation Authority.

SURP Researcher Featured in Diliman Research Magazine

UP SURP and NAPC Signs Statement of Commitment
Date Posted: February 6, 2026

On 04 February 2026, the University of the Philippines School of Urban and Regional Planning (UP SURP) and the National Anti-Poverty Commission (NAPC) formalized their partnership by signing a Statement of Commitment (SoC) to promote mutual advocacies and engage in cooperative actions. This collaboration aims at strengthening anti-poverty initiatives through meaningful and active involvement through technical assistance and knowledge-sharing about local planning and program development.

DSHUD and UP SURP Strengthen Partnerships with LGUs
Date Posted: January 29, 2026

On 24 January 2026, the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD), through the Environmental, Land Use, Urban Planning and Development Bureau (ELUPDB), in partnership with the UP School of Urban and Regional Planning (UP SURP), through its Office of Training and Extension Services (OTES), welcomed representatives from the local government units (LGUs) of Ambaguio, Nueva Vizcaya; Padre Burgos, Quezon; and Remedios T. Romualdez, Agusan del Norte. The activity marked the conduct of the SURP Refining Institutions through Skills Enhancement (SURP RISE) Program and the Orientation on Climate and Disaster Risk Assessment (CDRA), held at the DHSUD–ELUPDB Urban Lab in Quezon City.

UP SURP Welcomes the 6th Batch of SURP RISE Trainees
